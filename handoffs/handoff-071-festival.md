> …who he must now face to decide whether he will continue to be -- a KILLER OF MEN.

### He solved the Gen 22 ambiguity better than the note proposed

*"his opponent's burial rites"* — possessive, **two words shorter** than the offered fix (*"the opponent he killed"*), ambiguity gone, and the clarity holds without spelling anything out: sentence 2 already says every victory means killing, and *burial rites* say he is dead.

### The title drop: real gain, real cost

**Gain.** It moves the closing question from action to **identity** — what he *is*, not what he does — and "continue to be" implies he already is one, which "kill again" does not. It also makes the title a category of person rather than atmosphere. Jordan's statement is about being *seen as* the brute, so the identity question is thematically truer.

**Cost.** *"Continue to be"* is copula + helper + infinitive, the flabbiest construction available; the line stops landing (*KILL a-GAIN* is two stresses, *con-TIN-ue to BE a KILL-er of MEN* is six diffuse ones). It is no longer showable in thirteen minutes. The em dash breaks **Luke's own Sep 7 rule** ("an obvious AI thing"). And it is the fourth death-word, with the title already sitting above the field.

### Measured, not asserted — 63 sourced loglines with an identifiable title

Title in the first quarter: **18** (the press-kit convention). Middle: **3**. Last third: **2**, and both are false positives — one matched on scattered words, the other ends "…in Lionsgate's Warrior", a distributor sign-off. **Effectively none end on the title as the film's answer.** ALL-CAPS appears 4 times, all front-loaded. Rarity is not proof it fails, and none of the 63 were written for a field with the title directly above it — but there is no track record behind the move.

### Recommendation: "remain" — 64 words, the same count as Gen 22

> …who he must now face to decide whether he will remain a killer of men.

One word for three, a real verb instead of a copula, identity framing intact, implication intact, no dash, no caps.
