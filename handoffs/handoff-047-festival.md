# handoff-047-festival

## Where we left off

**This turn (Sep 7, 2026, night, Opus 5):** Luke asked for five or six *really good* ones, said the cost slot is the weakest, and asked to revisit Joan's enticement test — *"what's missing is the part that makes me want to watch that fucking movie."*

**The diagnosis, and the turn's actual finding:** no version had yet said the most enticing fact in the film — **he stumbles on the funeral of the man he killed, and the mourners are his own people, practising the faith he shares.** He is the murderer at the funeral, and the funeral is his own church. Every version had been ending on an abstraction (*reckons, confronts, humanity, roots*) when the enticing thing is his *position*: he cannot grieve, cannot join, cannot claim the faith being practised six feet from him, because he made the body.

**The cost slot rebuilt in three tiers** (`press/logline-enticing.md`): Tier 1 is the cost as a **right he has lost** — the right to mourn · to grieve · to claim the faith · a place in the ground beside them · to pray with them. Each names a specific barred action in a scene the reader can already see; "humanity" is a noun, "the right to mourn" is a closed door. Tier 2 is what he has become (hardened past recognition; consumes another piece; unrecognisable to his own dead). Tier 3 is standing (estranged, belongs to the men who own him) — the two he flagged as not enticing.

**Six delivered** at 40–65 words, each built on the collision and using his own picked words (will to live · what he is willing to do to stay alive · hardens · consumes · survives by killing his own · his C opening · his crux line). Recommendation: **1** or **6** stop a programmer because both end on the impossible position; **3** is the most uncomfortable; **4** preserves his crux line. The line to keep if only one survives: **"the only man there with no right to mourn."**

His enticement verdicts and picks logged as idea box Generation 12. The 59-word "whose physical dominance" version he asked about is carried as version A in `logline-top-versions.md` Part 1.
