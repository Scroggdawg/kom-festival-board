## What the documents say (page numbers are the handbook's own)

- Neither document mentions FilmFreeway, social media, or festival strategy. No role (director, producer, DP) is given authority over festivals.
- **AFI owns the film outright** (Enrollment Agreement §5(b); handbook p.84). Distribution and exploitation rights in perpetuity are AFI's. Credits are AFI-approved and cannot be altered by any Fellow (EA §5(b)(iv); handbook p.83 "as approved and in no other form").
- **Festival entries and award submissions before delivery need approval from the Sr. Manager, Post Production & Delivery** (Susan Dretzka, SDretzka@AFI.com) — handbook p.77 and p.84. Screenings before delivery, including for time-sensitive festivals, need the same approval (p.84).
- **The team names ONE "Project Designate" in the Production Fellows Agreement** to receive all information, correspondence and payments for awards and distribution (p.84). Revenue is shared per that agreement or split evenly. This is the only handbook rule about who is the festival contact. Who KOM designated is unknown.
- **Starting distribution before the 18-month window needs a written request signed by all team members** (p.84). Team-signature model.
- Production funds left after delivery must go to promotion, distribution, festivals, with standard cost reporting; anything left 18 months after delivery reverts to AFI (p.17).
- Credit policy treats the principal team as co-equals: same card size and duration for all principal Fellows, no "A film by" credit "due to the collaborative nature of the program", principal Fellows listed alphabetically in the crawl (p.81–83).
- **Production Intervention** (p.86–88): triggers include "failure to collaborate in a positive and productive manner" and "failure to respect the rights of team members." Informal path: team member → Thesis Mentor → Production Manager facilitates → written summary to Head of Production → follow-up within 5 days. Formal path: Discipline Heads + Associate Dean of Academic Affairs, meeting within a week, written decision within 48 hours, appeal to the Dean within 5 business days. Scoped to development through post production and to enrolled teams; Luke graduated 2025, so applicability is uncertain.
- **Complaints** (p.90): Office of Fellow Affairs, fellowaffairs@afi.edu. The Enrollment Agreement cover sheet points to the separate *Fellow Handbook* for "Emphasis on Collaboration" (p.30) and "Complaint Form and Process" (p.58). Luke does not have that document yet.
- The "Resolving Disputes" section (p.80) covers writing credits only.
- No exceptions to any handbook policy without the Dean's written authorization (front matter).
