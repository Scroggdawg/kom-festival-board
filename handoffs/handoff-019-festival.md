### What this overturns on the board

1. **Sundance went in.** Open question since handoff 016 — answered. Notification Dec 4.
2. **PAFF is not closed.** Board says close 2026-08-31; FilmFreeway shows a FINAL
   deadline of **Sep 16, 2026**, and it is already submitted. Two errors in one record.
3. **The premiere ledger is almost certainly wrong.** All 8 premieres read `available`.
   ABFF (Miami, ~Jun 2026) and MVAAFF (~Aug 2026) both selected the film. If it
   screened at either, the world, international, North American and US premieres were
   spent months ago and the whole sequencing apparatus is protecting an asset that no
   longer exists. **Not yet confirmed — "Selected" is not proof it screened. Luke is
   the only source for this.**
4. **Empirical hit rate:** 2 for 2 on premium Black-audience festivals (ABFF, MVAAFF);
   0 for ~7 on general prestige (TIFF, NYFF, Edinburgh, SITGES, New Orleans, Essence,
   Student Academy Awards). Small sample, but it is the only real signal on the board.
   Urbanworld is the counterexample — Black circuit, still a no.
5. **Raindance and SXSW London already said no**, yet both sit on the target lists with
   fresh deadlines (Dec 7 / Sep 30). Resubmission decision, not a new opportunity.
