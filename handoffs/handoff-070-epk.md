### The one judgement call, made and not hidden

The camera originals are **714 MB**, up to 53 MB a file. This repository publishes the board through GitHub Pages, and **a published Pages site may be no larger than 1 GB**. The repo is ~121 MB today; the originals would take it to ~835 MB with headshots and the trailer still to come. The delivery after next would stop the live board from building.

So the repo carries **all 102 at long edge 2560, quality 86 — 48 MB**. Page 8 of the EPK is a 1296×1728pt page; 2560 is generous for it, and the set is still a usable press-web size. **Nothing was dropped from the selection.** Only the pixel dimensions changed, and the README says so in the table and in its own section.

The originals stay at `FINAL/BTS Selects` on Luke's disk and belong in the Drive folder (`A.4`, still open). This is not a workaround — `press/epk-spec.md` already says the EPK is a **link hub** and that full-resolution assets live on Drive behind links. The repo holds what the PDF needs; Drive holds the masters.

I flagged repo weight in handoffs 068 and 069 and said the fix, when it bit, was a web-size set with masters on Drive. It bit here.

**Two open questions the files raised:**

1. **No photographer is named in any file.** Page 8 needs a credit line and the thanks list needs the name. Nobody has been asked.
2. **The May frames predate both July shoot days.** `KOM_Anamorphic*`, `KOM_Stage-*` and the Fuji frames are 21–23 May; the shoot is 4 and 8 July. They read as camera and lens tests, which is a guess from dates and filenames, not a fact. Worth one word from Jordan before any of them is captioned as set photography.
