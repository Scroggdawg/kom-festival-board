## CRITICAL

1. Publishing can erase newly staged changes—or commit discarded ones.

`publish()` awaits the remote read, computes `merged` from the mutable global `OPS`, awaits the PUT, then clears all of `OPS`. Editing and discarding remain enabled throughout.

Consequences:

- An operation staged after `merged` is computed is not included in the commit, but is erased at success.
- An operation discarded after `merged` is computed can still be committed.
- The commit description can describe a different operation set than the JSON content.

See [stage() at index.html:2834](</Users/scrogdawg/BMF Headquarters/Previous Years/2025 - SEW TO GROW/25_01 KILLER OF MEN (THESIS)/FESTIVAL CAMPAIGN/site/index.html:2834>), [merge at index.html:3382](</Users/scrogdawg/BMF Headquarters/Previous Years/2025 - SEW TO GROW/25_01 KILLER OF MEN (THESIS)/FESTIVAL CAMPAIGN/site/index.html:3382>), [PUT at index.html:3390](</Users/scrogdawg/BMF Headquarters/Previous Years/2025 - SEW TO GROW/25_01 KILLER OF MEN (THESIS)/FESTIVAL CAMPAIGN/site/index.html:3390>), and the unconditional clear at [index.html:3398](</Users/scrogdawg/BMF Headquarters/Previous Years/2025 - SEW TO GROW/25_01 KILLER OF MEN (THESIS)/FESTIVAL CAMPAIGN/site/index.html:3398>).

Fix: snapshot operation IDs at publish start, disable mutation while publishing, and remove only the exact committed IDs after success.

2. `publishForced()` is a lost-update/data-loss engine.

The conflict UI marks selected operations `force`, but `publishForced()` bypasses `prev` checks for every `setField`, `setPremiere`, and `setKeyDate` operation—not just conflicts the user reviewed. If upstream changes again while the conflict sheet is open, those new changes are overwritten without another review.

Worse:

- Missing festival/premiere/key-date targets are silently skipped.
- Other conflicting operations are applied twice identically, remain unapplied, and are then silently treated as success.
- The complete draft is cleared after that “success.”
- A 409 retries the same forced overwrite against a newer SHA without re-review.

See [conflict mutation at index.html:3422](</Users/scrogdawg/BMF Headquarters/Previous Years/2025 - SEW TO GROW/25_01 KILLER OF MEN (THESIS)/FESTIVAL CAMPAIGN/site/index.html:3422>), [forced application at index.html:3438](</Users/scrogdawg/BMF Headquarters/Previous Years/2025 - SEW TO GROW/25_01 KILLER OF MEN (THESIS)/FESTIVAL CAMPAIGN/site/index.html:3438>), the ineffective double application at [index.html:3445](</Users/scrogdawg/BMF Headquarters/Previous Years/2025 - SEW TO GROW/25_01 KILLER OF MEN (THESIS)/FESTIVAL CAMPAIGN/site/index.html:3445>), and clear/retry at [index.html:3453](</Users/scrogdawg/BMF Headquarters/Previous Years/2025 - SEW TO GROW/25_01 KILLER OF MEN (THESIS)/FESTIVAL CAMPAIGN/site/index.html:3453>).

Fix: remove `publishForced()`. Use one rebase loop that recomputes conflicts after every fetch/409. A “keep mine” decision must be bound to the exact upstream value and SHA the user reviewed.
