## §B. Joan's system, as stages (not tools)

Luke asked in tools twice ("Are you using this instead of Vimeo?"); Joan answered in stages both times ("No, no, no, no, let's just backtrack. This is before you get accepted into festivals."). The system is three stages plus a hub plus a ledger.

| Stage | Tool | What it holds | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B.1 Apply** | FilmFreeway | One project per version/purpose; attachments = student ID + EPK only; screenings & awards filled; accurate categories; credits = leads + recognisable names + composer + sound designer | "documents to festivals should be emailed they shouldn't be on film freeway" |
| **B.2 Be watched** | Vimeo | One upload per audience: Tier-A festivals, Tier-B festivals, a downloadable link (for festivals that require it at submission), press / friends-and-family (non-downloadable, so it "doesn't fuck up my analytics"), and variants (burned-in subtitles, special cuts) | "I should have created two from the get-go. One for like first tier film festivals and then B for second tier festivals." |
| **B.3 Deliver** | MyAirBridge (Dropbox also fine; not Google — a download came back corrupted; not WeTransfer — links expire) | Pre-built folder: DCP · EPK · Film stills & director photo · Film master (clean, no subtitles, 2K, MOV/MP4) · Poster · SRT · Synopsis, credits & director bio · Trailer. Per-festival share folders with a read-only link. Per-festival packages when needed: laureled poster, laureled trailer, localised subtitled screener, SRT, 60-second director intro | "my error bridge is for when you get accepted" |
| **B.4 Asset hub** | Google Drive | `05 MARKETING / 07 FESTIVALS`: Accepted Festivals · Festival Cover Letters · Festival Details · Festival Social Media Posts · Festival Submission Receipts · Festival Laurels · Instagram Posts. `00 PRESS`: BTS · HEADSHOTS · POSTER · STILLS · TRAILER · z_Old_EPKs · EPK INFO · EPK LINKS · FAQs · Social Toolkit. The EPK PDF links into these folders | "So you're basically using the EPK as a way to get them hooked up to the Google Drive? Yes." |
| **B.5 Ledger** | Spreadsheet + FilmFreeway list | Price per festival; rejected rows shaded darker; sorted by the next deadline whichever comes first — the end of a reduced-price tier or the final; a FilmFreeway list so reminders arrive; FilmFreeway's *Export Submissions* as the source of truth for outcomes | "you guys should include the price. That way you can keep track of how much you guys are spending." |

What Vimeo analytics can and cannot do: per-link views, unique viewers, average % watched, finishes, downloads, and region down to city — enough to tell whether a festival opened the link and roughly where from. Not enough to predict outcomes: "we got like a hundred percent view on aesthetic film festival and we didn't get in." Signal for follow-up, never a verdict.

## §G. What the call reveals beyond the to-dos

1. **The application is a second product, judged on its own.** Luke said this, not Joan. It converts a list of fixes into a standard, and it reframes rejections as feedback on the application rather than a verdict on the film. → §A
2. **Joan reads from the programmer's chair; the page was written from the inside.** Her test is never "is this accurate" — it is "why would a festival get excited." The page is accurate. Every edit rotates it to face the reader. → §C
3. **The thing an outside viewer remembers is the thing the page hides.** Unprompted, Joan names the surreal ancestral sequence as what sets the film apart. No still shows it; the logline mentions faith in passing; the spirituality paragraph is buried mid-statement. → §C.1, §C.3, §C.6
4. **Joan thinks in stages; Luke asked in tools.** Apply, be watched, deliver — with tools slotted in. The monitoring system should be organised that way. → §B
5. **One link per purpose is the measurement system, and the signal is noisy.** Adopt her regrets (split tiers on day one), not just her setup. → §B.2
6. **The campaign is custodian of none of its own assets.** Make the campaign, not individuals, the holder of the deliverables. → §F.2
7. **The discount email is a pitch, not a fee question.** It is the festival's first contact with the film. → §D.1
8. **Luke keeps a calendar; Joan keeps a ledger.** Cost, status and outcome per festival — the denominator is not currently tracked ("three of however many"). → §D.5
9. **Independence comes from the discipline, not the tools.** Sequence, ledger, one link per purpose, submit early, discount before paying. Joan kept handing ownership back: "this is your thing. This is yours." → §A
10. ~~The rejection history is partly about a different film~~ — withdrawn. KOM has only ever been 13 minutes; the 19-minute remark was about Joan's film. The rejection history is evidence about *this* film's application, which strengthens §G.1 rather than weakening it.
11. **Which instincts held and which were redirected.** Held: sort by next deadline and shortlist; leave the AFI showcase off; Vimeo with per-purpose links; content before layout; discount emails today; a dedicated delivery account. Redirected: the group photo; "everybody" in credits; "just change the date"; hunting for a named contact; a factual logline; festival forms as attachments. Most redirects were about the reader's perspective, not judgment.
