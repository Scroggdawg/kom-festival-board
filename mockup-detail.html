How it moves (the smooth part)

Open: phone — slides up as a bottom sheet with a spring, grab-bar to drag down; desktop — scales in from 97% with a fade, 200ms ease-out, board dims behind.
Status change: the header wash cross-fades to the new color and the new history dot drops in — one motion, 250ms, then still. Nothing pulses, ever.
Stage → publish: staging closes the sheet and the draft bar counts up; the sheet never traps you in a second modal.
Reduced motion: all of it swaps to plain cross-fades.
Markup only — fake data. Companion pages: mockup.html (rows, cards, timeline) · the live board is unchanged until approved.
