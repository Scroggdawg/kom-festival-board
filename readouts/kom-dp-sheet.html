DP Craft Sheet

The cinematographer's own campaign, run in parallel with the film's festival run: awards, cinematography festivals, and press that honor the images. Nothing on this sheet costs premiere status, almost nothing here charges a fee, and the two school-gated entries route through AFI, not the team.

Researched Aug 25, 2026. ~ marks a deadline estimated from the prior cycle.

The precedent

AFI class of 2024's Zach Morrison won the ASC Student Heritage Award for "The Witch Pricker and the Hair" — a 1690s Scotland period short built on single-source lighting — and got an American Cinematographer interview off the back of it. A chiaroscuro firelight period piece from AFI winning the ASC's student award is not a hope; it is the established pattern. This sheet exists to run that exact play.

Press channels

None of these are paid PR. They are entries and editorial pitches, and the pitch material is the lighting itself.

  • American Cinematographer — Rising Stars of Cinematography. Annual editorial feature (~9 emerging DPs, many shorts-only). Pitched or nominated, not bought. The pitch: lighting breakdowns of the single-source firelight work — how the barn, cabin and void sequences were built in 2.39:1 tenebrism. AC's remit explicitly includes shorts.
  • AFI Cinematography Spotlight. AFI's own showcase page for each graduating cinematography class, with reels and contacts. Confirm the 2026 class page entry is live and points at the current reel.
  • theasc.com coverage. Rides the Heritage Award — winners and finalists get ASC editorial. No separate action beyond winning the entry slot.
  • Frame grabs as the calling card. Every craft entry above wants stills; the strongest five frames (fight-room shaft light, hammer silhouette, lantern void, hearth two-shot, funeral tableau) should live in one folder at full 2K, graded, ready to attach.

The one blocking email

Camerimage Etudes and the ASC Heritage Awards only accept school submissions. The email to AFI's festival office (already on the campaign board's ask list) carries three DP questions: was KOM entered in ASC 2026; will AFI designate it for ASC 2027; will AFI submit it to Camerimage Etudes 2027 and confirm the academic-year eligibility with the festival.
