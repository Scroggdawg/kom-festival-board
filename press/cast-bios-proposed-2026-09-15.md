# Cast bios: proposed conservative revision

Proposed 15 September 2026, after re-opening every source that day. Not yet in the worksheet; field 7.1 still carries the 10 September text. Luke decides.

## The rule applied

A claim stays only if one of these backs it: an IMDb credit; a venue, institution or award body's own page; the actor's own official website; or a dated third-party organisation page. A claim goes if it rests only on a bio the actor wrote for a database (IMDb mini-bio, Second City blurb, NetworkISA) or on a self-maintained profile field (Backstage, Actors Access). Degrees, union memberships, birthplace and heritage are out unless documented. Anything cut can come back on the actor's own word.

## The writing pass

Each bio now runs three or four sentences, opens with who the person is, gives the work in one or two sentences, and ends on the film. Lists are in one breath; no sentence carries two "includes". Nothing is described that the reader cannot check.
