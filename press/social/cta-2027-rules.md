# 46th College Television Awards — nominee posting rules

Source: `46th_CTA_Nominees_Toolkit.pdf` (Television Academy Foundation, 4 pages, in Luke's Downloads; 125 MB, not committed). Laurels: `press/laurels/cta-2027/Found_CTA27_Laurel-Nominee_{Black,White}.png`, 1980 × 1295, transparent, byte-identical to the toolkit files.

## Laurel: where it may go
- On its own as an image, or on Killer of Men's **key art or production stills**. Nothing else: not BTS photos, headshots, title cards or other artwork. *(BTS counts as "other images" by the plain reading; ask the Foundation before using one.)*
- The toolkit's own OK examples post it as black on white and white on black.

## Laurel: what may not change
| Rule | Means in practice |
|---|---|
| Keep the ratio; no stretch or squeeze | Scale proportionally only: 1980 : 1295 (1.529 : 1) |
| No invert, flip, reshape, recolour | Use the black or white file as supplied; no tint, no red, no gold |
| No filters | No grain, glow, blur or drop shadow on the laurel |
| Background (laurel posted alone) | Keep the colour set as supplied; place it on plain white or black |
| No overlap | Must not touch other text or logos: the poster's title or billing block, the AFI logo |
| Size | **Laurel height no more than 1/5 of the whole image height** |

## Maximum laurel size by format
| Canvas | Max laurel height | Max laurel width |
|---|---|---|
| 1080 × 1350 (IG portrait) | 270 | 413 |
| 1080 × 1080 (square) | 216 | 330 |
| 1080 × 1920 (story / reel) | 384 | 587 |
| 1920 × 1080 (still, 16:9) | 216 | 330 |
| 2160 × 2700 (poster master) | 540 | 825 |

Our stills are 1920 × 1080 letterboxed (picture in rows 138–941). Measure the 1/5 against the **delivered image**, and keep the laurel inside the picture, not on the bars. The toolkit's OK example puts it in a corner, clear of the title.

## Naming
- Use: "Television Academy Foundation College Television Awards", "the College Television Awards", or "the 46th College Television Awards".
- Never: "College Emmys", "College TV Emmys", "annual College Television Awards".
- Hashtag: **#CollegeTVAwards**
- Tag: Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn **@TelevisionAcademyFoundation**; TikTok **@TVAcademyFoundation**
