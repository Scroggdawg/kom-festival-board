# Cast bios: annotated bibliography

Each bio in worksheet field 7.1 is broken into its pieces of text below, and each piece is cited to the source line that supports it, quoted as captured on 10 September 2026. Compiled 14 September 2026 from the captures in `press/cast-bios-evidence/`. Sandra McDaniels's paragraph is included after the three written bios; it is built from credits, as no bio of her exists.

**How each source was read.** "Page text" means the page's own text as read in the Browser pane (IMDb and Backstage refuse fetches). "Fetch extract" means the fetch tool's extraction of the page, which paraphrases in places. "Checkers' capture" means a page opened by one of the nine fact-check agents that afternoon and quoted in its verdict (`factcheck-verdicts.json`). Times are Central.
