## 1. Aspen Shortsfest — send first

**To:** shortsfest@aspenfilm.org
**Subject:** Fee waiver request, student thesis film: KILLER OF MEN (Aspen Shortsfest 2027)

Hello Aspen Shortsfest team,

My name is Luke Scroggins and I'm the cinematographer on KILLER OF MEN, a 13-minute AFI Conservatory thesis film by writer-director Jordan Betine. It's a Southern Gothic set in 1830s South Carolina: an enslaved man is forced to fight for his enslaver's profit, and the film stays inside the ring with him rather than out in the crowd. We are very proud of it and of what it has to say.

Shortsfest is one of the few festivals in the country where a short drama is taken as seriously as a feature, and the Jury Award for Drama is the exact lane we made this film for. We would be honored to bring it to Aspen.

I've read rule 5.a and understand your waivers are reserved for filmmakers who can't pay for political or geographical reasons. That isn't our case, and I won't pretend it is. Our case is simpler: we are recent graduates, a team whose roots are in Nigeria, China, India and the United States, running a festival campaign on a student budget, and the fees add up fast. If any student discount or partial reduction of the regular fee is possible, we would be deeply grateful. I'm writing inside your request window and before we submit, and we will submit either way.

The film was selected this year by the American Black Film Festival and the Martha's Vineyard African American Film Festival[, and has just been selected by the SCAD Savannah Film Festival].

Thank you for your time. We sincerely hope you enjoy the show.
