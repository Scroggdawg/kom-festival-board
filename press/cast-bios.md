## Draft bios

These are written from the verified facts below, not copied from any source, and were fact-checked claim by claim on Sep 10 (see Fact-check below). Each actor should approve their bio before print. They are in EPK field 7.1 and the EPK INFO doc.

**Erik Orjiako (Mace).** Erik Orjiako is a Swedish-born Nigerian American actor and writer based in Los Angeles. He studied English and communications at UMass Amherst and earned an MFA in screenwriting at the New York Film Academy. A graduate of Second City Hollywood's improv program, he has performed with its sketch team and written shows there. His credits include the series Minx, the short film Al Davis: Just Win Baby and the podcast series The Imperfection. He has also worked as a script reader and creative executive. In Killer of Men he plays Mace.

**Sandra McDaniels (The Elder).** Sandra McDaniels is a Los Angeles-based actress and a member of SAG-AFTRA. Her screen credits include the Apple TV+ series Truth Be Told, the short films Providence and 172 Push-Ups, and the film Gingersnap Christmas, in which she plays Gramma Belle. In Killer of Men she plays the Elder.
*No written bio for Sandra exists on IMDb, TMDB, Backstage or Project Casting. This draft is built from her credits only. Ask her, or NTA Talent Agency, for her own.*

**Eric Pargac (Master Kenner).** Eric Pargac is a Los Angeles-based actor and filmmaker who grew up in Texas. He was a founding artistic director of Furious Theatre Company in Los Angeles, where he performed in ten productions and shared StageSceneLA's 2006-2007 Lead Actor of the Year (Drama) award for An Impending Rupture of the Belly. He has also performed at Artists Repertory Theatre in Portland, Intiman Theatre in Seattle and the IRT Theater in New York. His television credits include The Late Show with Stephen Colbert and HBO's Succession. He starred in, co-directed and co-produced the web series The Digressions. He is a member of SAG-AFTRA and Actors' Equity. In Killer of Men he plays Master Kenner.

**Jamal Dennis (Kojo).** Jamal Dennis is a Los Angeles-based actor and stunt performer. His training includes technique and scene study at the Royal Academy of Dramatic Art and scene study with Lesly Kahn and Craig Archibald. He holds a black belt in martial arts and has trained in Kyokushin, Wushu, Ninjutsu and Shotokan. He played the lead, Lavelle Sanders, in the series Be Someone. His screen credits include The Young and the Restless, S.W.A.T., Marvel's Runaways, Zoo, Mississippi Grind and Glory Road, and he has performed stunts on Sinners, Emancipation and Grudge Match. He is a member of SAG-AFTRA. In Killer of Men he plays Kojo.

## Fact-check, Sep 10

Eight independent checkers, two per actor, each tried to refute every claim. One checked source by source; the other looked for overreach and misattribution. A ninth agent merged their verdicts. Changes it made to the drafts:

| Actor | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Erik Orjiako | "written and performed with its sketch team" became "performed with its sketch team and written shows there" | Second City supports performing with the sketch team; ISA supports writing shows at Second City, not for the sketch team |
| Erik Orjiako | "television special" became "short film" for Al Davis: Just Win Baby | The production company calls it a short film made for Super Bowl LVIII; no broadcast confirmed |
| Eric Pargac | "starred in ten productions" became "performed in ten productions" | His resume's ten Furious roles include supporting parts |
| Eric Pargac | "named Stage Scene LA's 2007 Actor of the Year" became "shared StageSceneLA's 2006-2007 Lead Actor of the Year (Drama) award" | The Scenies page lists him under Lead Actor of the Year/Drama (Tie) |
| Eric Pargac | "produced" became "co-produced" The Digressions; "HBO's" now attaches only to Succession; "IRT Theater" | Series page lists four producers; The Late Show is CBS; venue styling |
| Jamal Dennis | "studied at the Royal Academy of Dramatic Art" became "technique and scene study at" RADA | His resume lists it as technique and scene study, with no programme or dates |
| Sandra McDaniels | Credit list reordered for clarity | No fact changed; all claims held |

Still to confirm with the actors: Erik's MFA, birthplace and creative-executive work each rest on one profile he wrote himself. Eric's union membership comes from 2018 to 2020 material and his Los Angeles base from an undated crew page. Jamal's black belt and union membership are self-reported.
